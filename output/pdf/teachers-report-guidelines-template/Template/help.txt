Follow the following instruction to use the template smoothly.

1. Edit title.tex according to your need.
2. Edit preamble.tex. Just comment out the contents those you are not interested to add in your report.
3. Edit declaration, funny quote, abstract, acknowledgement, abbreviation, constants, symbols, dedication etc whatever you need to complete your report. 
4. Add contents in the main 6 chapters named introduction, artoftheworks, proposedMethod, implementations, experimentalResults and conclusion.
5. Edit appendix sources in main.tex to add appendices.
6. Add your references through Bibliography.bib file. some samples are given in the file. 
7. Keep all of your graphics in the Figures/ folder.
8. Have fun with the template changing according to your further need in the Thesis.cls file.



For further clarification, you can contact anyone you believe that can help you.
It will be appreciated if you modify the template for betterment and share with others.
