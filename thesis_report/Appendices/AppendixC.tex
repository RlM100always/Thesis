\section{Evidence Integrity Checklist}

Before final submission the authors will verify each item:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item Every empirical number links to a committed code version, dataset
  manifest and saved artifact.
  \item Synthetic, public-real and primary-real results appear in separate
  tables and are described with their permitted claims.
  \item No customer or later outcome crosses a training/test boundary.
  \item Encoders, imputers, scalers and thresholds are fitted only on training
  or validation data as appropriate.
  \item Every forecast model is compared with a relevant naive baseline on an
  untouched future period.
  \item Imbalanced tasks report PR-AUC, base rate, lift and calibration, not
  accuracy alone.
  \item SHAP explanations are generated from the final audited model and are not
  written as causal findings.
  \item Partner permissions, anonymization, retention and deletion are documented.
  \item Figures, tables and pseudocode are produced by the authors and every
  external idea, dataset or result has a verifiable citation.
  \item The final draft is reviewed under University of Dhaka policy, including
  the supervisor's similarity target and any required disclosure of AI assistance.
  \item Both authors can reproduce and explain every reported experiment and
  recommendation calculation.
\end{enumerate}
