\section{Role of the Two Analytics Surfaces}

The \texttt{/api/...} routes expose fixed thesis-research artifacts and an
anonymous exploratory upload flow. The \texttt{/api/app/...} routes operate on
tenant-scoped business records. This separation prevents an exploratory upload
from silently becoming a production organization model and enables research
results to remain reproducible while operational data change.
