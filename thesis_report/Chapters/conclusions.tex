\section{Research Summary}
\label{researchsummary}

This thesis addresses the gap between recording small-business transactions and
making defensible operational decisions from them. The proposed \system{}
framework connects an auditable retail operations layer, leakage-safe predictive
analytics, explanation, business constraints and action ranking. The system is
Bangladesh-oriented through its payment methods, calendar/context features,
currency, Bangla-first daily interface and primary data plan.

The current implementation is a working local MVP. It covers branch and tenant
isolation, products, inventory, sales, purchases, returns, finance, customers,
suppliers, durable imports, canonical exports, model training, an API, a web
dashboard and strategy recommendations. The codebase passes its current six-test
backend suite and produces a valid production frontend build.

The strongest current evidence comes from public real data. A task-aligned
daily-demand benchmark selects a model within each development period and
obtains fixed-holdout WAPE of 17.49\% on the Bangladesh retailer
series and 30.37\% on UCI Online Retail II. UCI future-repeat prediction reaches
PR-AUC 0.795 and ROC-AUC 0.749. On WFP/DAM Bangladesh commodity prices,
last-value forecasts win three of four series and SARIMA wins one. Complexity
must therefore be earned by out-of-time evidence.

The controlled synthetic results provide a second methodological lesson. A
legacy segment score of 94.69\% is invalid because CLV nearly reconstructs the
label. With a new untouched 1,000-customer test, training-only feature selection
and model-family choice, selected XGBoost obtains 69.90\% accuracy and 71.52\%
macro-F1. Synthetic churn reaches 2.67x top-decile lift, while return prediction
remains weak. Negative results are retained rather than concealed.

The report does not conclude that the system improves real SME performance yet.
That claim requires consenting invoice and stock histories, calibrated
per-business tests, an owner study and strategy impact measurement. The present
contribution is a technically complete platform and a scientifically honest path
to that evidence.

\section{Future Work Plan}
\label{futureplans}

\subsection{Priority 1: Ethics and Primary Data}

The immediate task is to finalize the target retail vertical, ethics submission,
consent form, data-sharing agreement and data dictionary. Three to five partners
will be recruited. Each file will pass provenance, privacy, key, monetary,
stock/return reconciliation, missingness and date-range checks before modelling.

\subsection{Priority 2: Final Leakage-Safe Experiments}

Demand models will use rolling or final-period evaluation. The baseline ladder
will include last-value, seasonal-naive and statistical methods before gradient
boosting or sequence models. Future-repeat snapshots will be horizon-purged and
evaluated with PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, Brier score, calibration and lift. Results will be
reported per business and under leave-one-business-out transfer. Corrected SHAP
artifacts will be generated only from the final leakage-safe models.

\subsection{Priority 3: Ablation, Scalability and Strategy Value}

Ablations will remove calendar, market-price, price/promotion and customer
features and will compare global with business-specific models. Scalability runs
will extend through one million synthetic rows with repetitions and uncertainty.
The reorder policy will be evaluated on identical demand paths against owner or
standard baselines using stock-out and holding costs. Association-rule
minimum-support experiments will be conducted only if market-basket analysis is
implemented within the final scope.

\subsection{Priority 4: Owner-Centered Evaluation and Hardening}

Representative users will complete operational and decision tasks with and
without concise explanation cards. The study will record completion, time,
errors, confidence and usability. Engineering work includes broader failure and
concurrency tests, frontend code splitting, backup/restore, calibration/drift
views and versioned outcome tracking. OAuth, statutory accounting and native
offline mobile support remain optional extensions unless required for the study.

\section{Expected Final Outputs}

By February 2027 the project aims to deliver:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item a reproducible thesis and source repository with a 40+ work literature
  review and verified references;
  \item a documented de-identified primary SME dataset or, if recruitment is
  constrained, a transparent account of collected scope and limitations;
  \item leakage-safe demand and future-repeat benchmarks with calibration,
  ablation and per-business transfer analysis;
  \item a tested constraint-aware strategy policy with stock-out/holding-cost
  simulation or pilot outcomes;
  \item usability results for owner-facing explanations;
  \item a hardened local demonstration, final diagrams, experiment artifacts
  and defence presentation; and
  \item a publication-ready manuscript focused on the strongest verified
  contribution, most likely leakage-safe low-data decision support for
  Bangladeshi retail SMEs.
\end{enumerate}

The final submission will retain negative results and evidence labels. If primary
data volume is insufficient for a deep model or a field impact claim, the thesis
will narrow the claim instead of expanding the conclusion beyond the evidence.
