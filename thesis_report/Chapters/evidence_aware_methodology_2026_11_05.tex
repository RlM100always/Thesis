\section{Evidence-Aware Integration and Comparative Evaluation Protocol}
\subsection{Four-Level RQ1 Forecast Evaluation}
For a common evaluation set \(I\) of individual shop--category--day observations, define WAPE after aggregation at level \(g\) as
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{WAPE}_g=
\frac{\sum_{u\in g}\left|\sum_{i\in u}y_i-\sum_{i\in u}\widehat y_i\right|}
{\sum_{u\in g}\left|\sum_{i\in u}y_i\right|}.
\end{equation}
The comparison holds the fitted model and test rows fixed, and changes only the grouping \(u\): network/day, shop/day, category/day or shop--category/day. It is a diagnostic of error cancellation rather than four independent model fits. Predictions must be formed before the absolute value is applied; no future outcomes are permitted in the features.

\subsection{Sparsity, Heterogeneity and Missing Stock Evidence}
Quantify observed zero-sales share, series mean and coefficient of variation, category-level and shop-level WAPE, and the difference between the sum of leaf absolute errors and the sum of daily aggregate absolute errors. At the all-zero-sales subset, WAPE is mathematically undefined: report MAE instead. Do not infer that observed zero sales represent true zero demand because stockouts may censor sales. Error-versus-zero-rate associations are descriptive and partly mechanical through denominator volume; no causality should be implied without an independent stock-availability signal.

\subsection{Reproducible Model Comparisons and Temporal Robustness}
Retain the original LightGBM and moving-average baselines as fixed comparators. Evaluate a two-stage hurdle candidate (probability of a positive observed sale multiplied by predicted conditional positive sale quantity) and an XGBoost regressor on the same features and the same observation-level error metric. Report failures to outperform the baseline. Additional earlier rolling-origin windows assess stability. Since candidate choices were considered after reviewing the original October--December test, comparisons on that period are \emph{exploratory}; future untouched or external validation is required for confirmatory ranking.

\subsection{Model Explanations and Human Evaluation}
LightGBM TreeSHAP predictions can be aggregated over a fixed sampled evaluation set, reporting mean absolute contributions in model raw-score units. A feature attribution is not a causal effect. The owner-facing action should disclose snapshot cutoff, evidence, missing data, uncertainty, and business constraints, but technical attribution alone is not proof of human usability. A future counterbalanced human study should compare no-explanation and explanation-assisted conditions on decision correctness, time, confidence and recognition of infeasible actions, with consent and institutional ethics requirements.

\subsection{Decision Evaluation and Integrity Gates}
A lead-time or multi-day demand forecast and a verified opening stock/receipt ledger are necessary to close the RQ1--RQ3 loop. The existing one-day ahead forecast must not be silently used as a validated 30-day demand model. Scenario-only oracle-demand policy results stay separate from an actual historical replay or business pilot. Similarly, an inactivity score does not estimate treatment uplift; intervention and consenting customer contact require separate validation. Researcher-supplied exports, even after arithmetic reconciliation, do not independently certify merchant origin or permission. All source versions and model results require source manifests and no customer-level data in public GitHub.
