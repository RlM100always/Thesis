Small retail businesses in Bangladesh routinely create sales, inventory,
payment and customer records, yet many lack an integrated mechanism for
turning those records into forecasts and practical decisions. This thesis
proposes \system{}, a Bangladesh-focused framework that connects daily business
operations with leakage-aware machine learning, explainability and
constraint-aware action ranking. The intended outputs are SKU demand
forecasts, future customer inactivity scores, customer segments, performance
indicators and recommendations such as reorder or retention priorities.

As of \reportdate, a local end-to-end minimum viable product is implemented.
It supports organization and branch isolation, catalogue and inventory
management, sales, purchases, returns, expenses, ledgers, durable CSV/XLSX
imports, model training, a FastAPI service and a React dashboard. The research
pipeline includes supervised and unsupervised segmentation, LSTM and ARIMA
forecasting with a seasonal-naive baseline, churn and return prediction, SHAP
analysis, and chronological real-data validation. Six automated backend tests
pass and the production frontend builds successfully.

Current experiments are real-data first. Under an identical daily-demand target,
expanding-window selection and fixed chronological holdouts, dataset-specific
models obtain 17.49\% WAPE on a 1,826-day real Bangladeshi retailer series and 30.37\%
on UCI Online Retail II. On UCI customer snapshots, 90-day future-repeat
prediction reaches ROC-AUC 0.749, PR-AUC 0.795 and 1.77x top-decile lift. A
separate WFP/DAM Bangladesh price benchmark gives holdout WAPE between 3.14\%
and 7.85\% for the winning method across four staple series; simple last-value
forecasts win three. Controlled generated-data experiments are reported only as
comparisons: development-only tuning selects XGBoost at 69.90\% segment holdout
accuracy (95\% CI [67.0\%, 72.6\%]), synthetic churn reaches 2.67x lift, and
return prediction remains weak (PR-AUC 0.083). These outcomes expose why
leakage control, untouched tests and strong baselines matter more than selecting
a model after observing favourable test accuracy.

These are prototype and external-validation results, not final field claims.
The primary unfinished dependency is consenting invoice-level data from
Bangladeshi SMEs. Work through February 2027 therefore focuses on ethics and
data agreements, primary data collection, calibrated temporal evaluation,
ablation and scalability studies, owner usability testing, business-impact
simulation or pilot evaluation, and final thesis consolidation.

\textbf{Keywords:} Bangladesh SMEs, business analytics, demand forecasting,
customer segmentation, churn prediction, explainable AI, decision support.
